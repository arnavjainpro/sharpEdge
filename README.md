# sharpEdge

A personal trading research and decision-support engine. It scans a **~1,500-stock liquid universe across the entire US market** (large/mid/small cap, sector-tagged) for both **long and short** setups, tracks **market regime and sector rotation**, validates every idea through a conservative multi-factor framework with **stress tests**, analyzes **intraday setups from chart screenshots or live data**, scores **any of ~12,000 searchable US stocks and ETFs** on demand — in the scanned universe or not — alongside its news, and turns everything into plain-English advice with the full pro detail one tap away. Set a price or score alert on anything you're watching.

> ⚠️ Decision-support tool, not licensed financial advice. Every trade is your decision.

## What it does

- **Full-market screener (no AI cost):** every 6 hours, pulls a year of daily prices for the filtered universe (market cap ≥ $300M, price ≥ $3, volume ≥ 300k, up to 3,000 names by default — all configurable) and computes trend structure, golden/death crosses, RSI, MACD, momentum, ATR, swing-pivot support/resistance, breakout/breakdown state with volume confirmation, relative strength vs sector ETF and SPY, and 60-day beta. Everything fuses into **separate 0–100 long and short scores** — momentum is capped so it can never dominate, and shorts are hard-capped below setup grade without confirmed structural breakdown. Fetches run in a **bounded-concurrency pool** (default 6, auto-throttles if the data source pushes back), so a 3,000-name scan finishes in a few minutes rather than tens.
- **Market regime + sector rotation (no AI cost):** trend-and-volatility regime with a risk-off flag, market breadth, and per-sector rotation states (leading / improving / weakening / lagging vs SPY) from the 11 SPDR sector ETFs. The Market tab shows the **real indices — Dow, S&P 500, Nasdaq, Russell 2000, plus VIX** — each one a click away from its TradingView chart. (The regime, relative-strength, and breadth math still runs on SPY/QQQ/IWM candles underneath; the indices are the display layer, not the inputs.) Ideas are presented **by sector**, and every AI judgment receives this context.
- **Idea validation (the core):** any ticker — screener candidate or your own idea, long or short — goes through one unified pipeline: deterministic evidence gathering (technicals, levels, RS, sector rotation, regime, 14-day news, earnings proximity, risk-first entry/stop/target frame, account-aware position sizing) → a deep-reasoning AI pass that scores six dimensions independently (technical, catalyst, market fit, news/sentiment, risk/reward, invalidation clarity), **stress-tests the idea** (base/bull/bear/failure cases + "does it survive risk-off?"), and rates it **strong / moderate / weak / reject** under a strict rubric. Weak setups get downgraded even if the chart looks exciting.
- **Intraday analyzer:** paste a chart screenshot and/or give a ticker + timeframe (1m/5m/15m/1h). It fetches live intraday bars, VWAP, opening range, relative volume, higher-timeframe structure, market tape, and same-day news, cross-checks the screenshot against real data, runs a 1-minute noise check (scalp vs momentum vs trend-continuation vs no-trade), and returns a full plan: entry zone, stop, targets, invalidation, holding period, confidence, R:R, exit-early conditions, and sizing. "No trade" is a first-class answer.
- **Options support:** every idea validation pulls the live chain (a strike ladder with bid/ask/IV per strike, ATM IV, implied move) and proposes **one concrete structure from the full menu** — long call/put, vertical debit/credit spreads, straddle, strangle, iron condor, covered call, cash-secured put, or calendar — chosen to fit both the directional thesis and the IV regime (sell premium when IV is rich, buy it when it's cheap, non-directional structures for range/big-move theses). The AI proposes real legs from the strike ladder; **max loss, max gain, and breakevens are computed deterministically by the Black-Scholes engine**, never taken on the model's word. IV-crush and theta risk are spelled out in plain terms.
- **Swing / options mode (Analyze tab):** upload 1-day / 1-week / 1-month candlestick charts (any subset) for a ticker — plus, optionally, a screenshot of your broker's options chain to price against those exact quotes. The model reads the multi-timeframe structure against live daily data and week-long news sentiment, then proposes a concrete **options structure** — strike, expiry, and spreads/straddles/strangles/iron condors chosen from the live strike ladder — whose **max loss, max gain, and breakevens are computed deterministically by a Black-Scholes engine** (spot × IV × time stress grid), never taken on the model's word.
- **In-trade management:** every analysis card has a follow-up chat — once you're in a position, ask "hit my first target, trim or hold?" and attach fresh screenshots. It re-pulls the current tape and answers against your original plan.
- **Backtest & walk-forward (Backtest tab):** describe a strategy in plain English (or upload a chart) — an AI translates it into a rule spec, and a deterministic engine backtests it on real daily candles with next-bar-open fills (no lookahead), then stress-tests it (worst historical window + 1,000-path Monte Carlo) and runs **anchored walk-forward validation**: grid-searches parameters on training windows only and reports **out-of-sample** results with a walk-forward-efficiency verdict, so you can tell a real edge from a curve-fit. Options backtests are model-priced (no historical option-quote feed exists) and labeled as such.
- **Brokerage integration:** link **Robinhood** (read-only — **Settings → Brokerage**, or `bun run link:robinhood` from a terminal) for live equity and options positions with 60-second updates during market hours (crypto is deliberately ignored — this is an equity/options tool). A transient broker failure never downgrades the dashboard to the YAML fallback; the last good snapshot keeps serving; or paste a JSON export from any broker in the Import panel. Priority: Robinhood > import > `portfolio.yaml` (the fallback). YAML theses merge onto broker positions. Read-only throughout — sharpEdge never places or cancels orders.
- **Search any ticker (⌘K / Ctrl-K):** type any symbol — **every US stock and ETF (~12,000), plus class shares** like `BRK.B` (dots, dashes, and slashes all resolve to the same name) — in the scanned universe or not, and get its live price, **your** long/short score and direction verdict, a 90-day sparkline, and the last week of company news, with a one-click jump to the TradingView chart. Off-universe symbols are scored **on demand** by rerunning the same screener pipeline over a year of daily candles, so the number you see is the number the scanner would give it, not an approximation. Anything with under ~1 year of history returns price + news and says why it has no score instead of faking one.
- **Price & score alerts:** from any ticker's detail panel, set *price above / below X* or *score ≥ N* (score alerts test the **higher of the long and short score** — the strength of the setup in whichever direction it's leaning). Alerts ride the live detector cycle (~90s while the market is open, 5 min extended, 30 min closed) and fire **once**, on a genuine crossing — one you create while the condition is already true stays quiet until the value leaves and crosses back in, so you don't get an instant useless ping. Firing retires the alert — or tick **repeat** and it re-arms itself, pinging again on each fresh crossing (never spamming while the condition stays true). Up to 20 tickers under watch. Quotes come from a **shared 60-second cache** that every read path uses (alerts, detectors, dashboard, AI context) and that the live trade websocket patches in real time, so a ticker you hold *and* alert on costs one Finnhub call, not several, and anything streaming on the websocket is priced at zero API cost. Delivery is Telegram (see Setup).
- **Accounts (multi-user):** email/password login gates the whole app; each user gets their own portfolio, broker link, saved ideas, trade journal, risk settings, and alerts, while market-wide data (screener, regime, universe) is shared. Sessions are server-side (SQLite) so logout is instant. The background monitoring pipeline watches one primary account (the first to sign up, which inherits any pre-existing single-user data).
- **Trade journal → personalized AI:** log how each closed trade actually went — win/loss/breakeven, P&L, and free-text notes on what went right or wrong. Relevant past trades (recent overall + any on the ticker being analyzed) are fed into future idea validations and chat as context, so the AI flags your recurring mistakes. Retrieval-as-context, not model fine-tuning.
- **Your risk profile:** a Settings tab holds max risk per trade, max position size, account equity (live from your broker by default; type a value to deliberately override it — e.g. to size against only the slice you trade — and "Reset to broker" to go back), and your **target risk/reward** — the minimum R:R a setup must clear to rate "strong." Raise it and the validator grades every idea more harshly.
- **Tabbed dashboard:** Portfolio · Market · Ideas · Analyze · Backtest · Chat · Activity · History · Settings, deep-linkable via URL hash, with unread badges on background tabs. A ⌘K search opens any ticker's quick panel, and its "Full analysis" jumps to a dedicated **stock detail page** (`#stock/TICKER`) with the full factor breakdown, past analyses, and a one-tap validate. Ideas group into a **collapsible sector tree** with a ⚙ **Filters popup**: one rule builder over any metric (score, market cap, `RSI < 35`, `% vs 200-day > 0`, relative strength, ATR, volume trend…) with `at least` / `above` / `below` / `at most`, shown as removable chips and saveable as named presets. The Market tab keeps the macro view (regime, indices, rotation) and links each sector's setups into Ideas instead of duplicating the ticker lists. The Portfolio tab splits **stocks and options into their own sections** and has a one-tap **"Score my portfolio"** AI health check (0–100 with pros, cons, biggest risk, next steps), and every stock page's news section has a **"Summarize"** button that digests the week's headlines into a few bullets on the cheap model. The Activity tab filters events by category (AI signals / setups / news / filings / price & volume / earnings / options expiry) with an "important only" toggle. Holdings carry **time-critical chips**: days-to-expiry on options (with a Telegram warning at 7 days and again at 1 day) and an "earnings Thu" chip when a holding reports within a week. Star any stock (**★ Watch** on its page) to manage the watchlist from the UI, and the **History tab** is one time-sorted feed of everything you've run — validated ideas, chart analyses, portfolio scores, and backtests — filterable by kind, paginated, with each ticker row linking to its full stock page. Portfolio scores are saved automatically (with a score-over-time trend on the Portfolio tab) and deletable. Beneath the feed sit the **trade journal** and two reference tables: validator calibration and the **idea scoreboard**, which replays every validated idea against real candles (stop hit = loss, first target = win) so you can see whether "strong" ratings actually earn their name. **Dark/light theme** follows your OS by default; Settings → Appearance overrides it. Your theme, active filters, and which sectors you collapsed persist across refreshes; expanded rows survive the 60-second live update (they reset on a page reload).
- **Live event monitoring:** quotes/volume anomalies via Finnhub websocket + polling, SEC filings via EDGAR, company news, earnings surprises — on your positions and watchlist, plus a 15-minute full-universe sweep that promotes any abnormal mover into live monitoring within minutes.
- **Two-tier AI:** Haiku triages every event for pennies; the deep model (Sonnet 5 by default — ~40% cheaper than Opus at near-Opus quality; override with `SHARPEDGE_MODEL_DEEP`) writes full signals only for events that matter. Notifications fire **only** for buy/sell advice.
- **Cost controls:** "Live AI updates" toggle stops all background token spend; circuit breakers hard-stop runaway AI calls; request queue throttles bursts. Screener, regime, rotation, and levels are pure math — $0.

## Setup

1. Supabase project: create one at https://supabase.com and copy the Postgres connection string (Project → Settings → Database → Connection string → URI)
2. Free Finnhub key: https://finnhub.io
3. Anthropic API key: https://platform.claude.com (needs credits)
4. `cp .env.example .env` and fill in `DATABASE_URL`, `FINNHUB_API_KEY`, and the Anthropic key — the schema is created automatically on first boot (migrating from an old local SQLite build? run `bun run scripts/migrate-to-supabase.ts` once)
5. Put your real holdings in `config/portfolio.yaml` (or link Robinhood / use the Import panel) — add a one-line `thesis:` per holding and a `risk:` section for sizing
6. `bun install && bun start` → http://localhost:3000
7. **Create an account** on first load. The first signup inherits the `portfolio.yaml` holdings and any existing data. Every account gets its own portfolio, broker link, and its own background monitoring — detectors, triage, alerts and the 9:00/16:15 briefings all run per account, against that account's positions. Each ticker is still fetched once no matter how many accounts hold it, so adding a user costs AI tokens but not market-data calls. Note the flip side: **every account with holdings generates AI spend**, so delete test accounts rather than leaving them signed up.

**Email verification (optional, recommended if the app is reachable from the internet):** set `RESEND_API_KEY` (free key from https://resend.com, 3k/month) in `.env`. This gates two flows, both a 6-digit code valid for 15 minutes with 5 guesses before it's discarded:
  - **Signup.** The account is staged in `pending_signups`, not `users` — the row that drives monitoring and AI spend is created only when the right code comes back. Without the key, signup creates the account immediately as it always did.
  - **Changing your sign-in email (Settings, SHARP-17).** The new address is staged on the account as `pending_email`, not written to `email`, until its code comes back — a typo or a hijacked session can't move the login. Gated on your current password too, so both proofs (you own the account, you can read the new inbox) are required. Without the key, the email field goes read-only in Settings rather than offering a change that can't be verified.

  Resend's default sender (`onboarding@resend.dev`) needs no DNS setup but only delivers to your own Resend signup address; verify a domain and set `EMAIL_FROM` to reach anyone else.

**Telegram (required for alerts to reach you):** set `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` (bot via @BotFather) and `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` (from @userinfobot) in `.env`. Without it, price/score alerts still evaluate and fire correctly but have nowhere to land — the only other channel is a native macOS notification, which off macOS just fails and writes a `[notify:mac] failed:` line to the log on every delivery. Signals (buy/sell advice) use the same channel.

Optional: Robinhood link (read-only, stores tokens locally). Do it in the dashboard under **Settings → Brokerage**: enter your Robinhood email and password, then approve the login in the Robinhood app or type the SMS/email code when it asks. The terminal equivalent is `bun run link:robinhood`, which additionally asks which sharpEdge account email to link to. Robinhood has no official public API; this uses the same private endpoints robin_stocks does (including the device-approval workflow) and can break without notice — the app degrades to import/YAML if so.

## Config files

- `config/portfolio.yaml` — holdings (shares, cost basis, thesis), watchlist, and `risk:` (account equity fallback, max risk per trade %, max position %). Held/watched tickers get real-time news/filing monitoring.
- `config/screener.yaml` — universe `filters:` (min market cap / price / volume, max stocks) plus extra tickers to force into the scan. These gate **what gets scanned**, not what's searchable: every US stock and ETF is stored and searchable regardless, and `max_stocks` only caps the ~1,500 names the 6-hour scan actually walks. ETFs are stored but never auto-scanned unless you hold or watch one (dollar-volume ranking would otherwise let SPY/QQQ crowd out real setups).

## Architecture

```
NASDAQ feeds: stocks (~7000 + sector/industry) + ETFs (~5200) ─► ~12k stored & searchable
                    ─► liquidity filter sets in_scan (~1500 liquid names actually scanned)
Yahoo daily OHLCV ─► screener: long/short confluence scores, S/R, RS, beta ─► setup events
Yahoo benchmarks (SPY/QQQ/IWM/VIX + ^DJI/^GSPC/^IXIC/^RUT + 11 sector ETFs)
                    ─► regime + sector rotation + breadth   (math on SPY; indices are the display row)
Finnhub ws+REST ─► shared 60s quote cache (ws trades patch it live) ─► every price read
Finnhub + SEC EDGAR ─► detectors: news/filings/moves ─► events
                    events ─► Haiku triage ─► deep-model signal (regime-aware)
                    detector cycle (~90s open) ─► alert crossing check ─► Telegram (fire-once)
on-demand: idea validator (evidence → 6-dim scoring → stress tests → strong/moderate/weak/reject)
           ticker lookup ⌘K (any symbol → same screener pipeline → score + news; 60s cache,
                             backs off during a scan, never writes the screener table)
           intraday analyzer (screenshot + live bars + HTF + tape → plan or no-trade)
           options context (chain IV, implied move) · advisor chat · briefings (9:00/16:15 ET)
broker: Robinhood link > JSON import > portfolio.yaml ─► positions/orders/equity ─► risk-first sizing
                    ─► dashboard (SSE) · notifications (buy/sell only) · SQLite
```

## API surface (all local)

- `GET /api/market` — regime, benchmarks (incl. Dow/S&P/Nasdaq/Russell/VIX), sector rotation boards
- `GET /api/screener` — every scanned stock with long/short scores + indicators
- `GET /api/ticker?sym=AAPL` — on-demand score + indicators + week of news for **any** ticker, in the scan universe or not. Also accepts index symbols (`^GSPC`, `^DJI`, `^IXIC`, `^RUT`), which get scored but carry no news. Dots and slashes normalize to dashes (`BRK.B` → `BRK-B`). 60s cache; returns 400 on a bad symbol, and 503 only for a *never-seen* symbol while a full scan is running — anything cached keeps serving through the scan, stale rather than dead
- `GET /api/alerts` — every alert, active and fired
- `POST /api/alerts` `{ticker, kind: price_above|price_below|score_gte, threshold}` — create an alert (seeded so an already-true condition won't fire instantly)
- `DELETE /api/alerts?id=N` — delete an alert
- `POST /api/ideas/validate` `{ticker, direction?: long|short|auto, notes?, options?}` — full validation report
- `POST /api/ideas/generate` `{count?}` — validate the strongest sector-diversified setups (capped at 6)
- `POST /api/intraday/analyze` `{ticker?, timeframe?, image?, notes?, options?}` — intraday plan
- `GET /api/ideas` — recent validation reports
- `POST /api/news/summarize` `{ticker}` — AI digest of the last 7 days of headlines (fast model)
- `POST /api/portfolio/score` — AI portfolio health check: 0–100 score with pros/cons/biggest risk
- `GET/POST /api/broker/*` — status, refresh, JSON import, and the Robinhood link flow (`link`, `link/status`, `link/code`, `unlink`)
- plus `/api/state`, `/api/quotes`, `/api/ask` (chat), `/api/briefing`, `/api/ai-live`, `/api/breaker/reset`

## Notes

- First full scan after boot takes ~15–30 minutes (1,500 tickers, rate-limited politely; shrink `filters.max_stocks` for faster passes). The market/sector view appears within ~1 minute.
- Typical AI cost: ~$0.10–0.50/day with live updates on; each idea validation or intraday analysis is a few cents (user-initiated).
- Shorting has costs this system does not model (borrow fees, locates, squeeze risk) — it flags the risk, you own the decision.
- Reset all history: truncate the tables in your Supabase project (the datastore moved from local SQLite to Postgres — there is no `data/sharpedge.db` file any more).
