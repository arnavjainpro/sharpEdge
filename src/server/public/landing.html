Why sharpEdge

Read-only, always

sharpEdge never places or cancels a broker order. Link Robinhood for live positions and the app only ever reads them.

The math is free

Screener, regime, sector rotation and support/resistance are pure deterministic math. No tokens, no cost, nothing to hallucinate.

Learns your mistakes

Log how each closed trade actually went. Relevant past trades feed future validations, so the AI flags the errors you keep repeating.

Your own account

Every account gets its own portfolio, broker link, journal and alerts. Market data is shared — your positions never are.

United States (English)

Decision-support tool, not licensed financial advice. Every trade is your decision.

Market data is sourced from Yahoo Finance, Finnhub and SEC EDGAR and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Scores, ratings and stress tests are model output, not predictions. Backtested and walk-forward results are hypothetical and do not represent actual trading; options backtests are model-priced because no historical option-quote feed is used.

Brokerage integration is read-only. Robinhood has no official public API and the link can break without notice, in which case the app falls back to imported or configured positions.
